4. Proof points

More results brands reported after unifying stores and online on Shopify. Every figure links to its source.

EY: sales uplift
8.9%
Equivalent uplift in sales for businesses on Shopify POS, on average. Based on transaction data from 3,800+ businesses and a survey of 600+ retail leaders. EY POS Market Report
Why it worksRunning POS and ecommerce as one system removes up to 60% of the middleware and upkeep, and makes omnichannel orders simpler. One retailer cut a three-step order process to one action.
EY: known-customer order value
up to 3x
Customers recognised at checkout spend up to 3x as much per order as anonymous shoppers. Shopify / EY
Why it worksShopify POS captures contact details at checkout, turning 8 in 10 first-time store shoppers into known customers. Staff and marketing can then recognise them and tailor what they offer.
EY: in-store growth from known customers
76%
Share of in-store sales growth that comes from known customers. They also drive up to 61% of repeat purchases. Shopify / EY
Why it worksOnce a shopper is known, their in-store and online purchases sit in one profile, so the brand can bring them back with relevant offers and rewards.

Online and halo effect

+59% market revenue
Representstreetwear. Revenue in the LA market grew 59% YoY after the brand opened its LA store on Shopify POS. Represent describes it as a "halo effect": people who saw the store looked the brand up online.
Why it workedSeeing the LA store made people look the brand up online. Store and warehouse stock are shared in real time, so shoppers can order online and pick up in store, or try on in store and have it shipped home.
2,000+ orders / month
Aviator Nationalready on Plus. Ships more than 2,000 online orders a month from store inventory, some of them same day.
Why it workedBefore the switch, the website couldn't sell store stock or accept online returns of in-store purchases. With every store on Shopify POS, online orders are filled from store inventory, and staff see each customer's full purchase history.
+74% online sales
Bentley125+ stores. Online sales up 74% and POS transactions up 17%. Bentley also replatformed online.
Why it workedReal-time stock across 125+ stores means online shoppers can see which nearby store has an item, and staff can send customers to another store when something is out. That made pickup in store and ship-from-store work.
+44% customer value
PAIGEapparel · 20+ stores. Customers who shop in store and online are worth 44% more than single-channel shoppers. During the holidays, 17% of online orders shipped from stores. "Being able to ship customer orders from store locations closest to them that have the inventory was amazing for our business." (Sofie Kuehnen, Head of Global Retail Operations). PAIGE moved its online store at the same time.
Why it workedStores and ecommerce became one team on one platform. Associates use clienteling to see what a customer browsed, bought and left in their cart online. Inventory accuracy rising from 85% to 98% made pickup in store and ship-from-store reliable.
40% higher LTV
Astrid & Miyu. Repeat customers who shop in store and online have 40% higher lifetime value than online-only customers.
Why it workedOne view of each customer across search, browsing, purchases and loyalty, online and in store. Marketing and rewards follow the customer wherever they shop, which brings omnichannel shoppers back.

In-store sales

+15% sales
Slam Jamstreetwear. Average daily orders up 15% after turning on in-store omnichannel with Shopify POS.
Why it workedStores became shipping hubs. If a size isn't in store, staff ship the right one home instead of losing the sale. In-store iPads capture customer data for marketing and double as self-serve kiosks.
+40% revenue
Monosonline-first. Year-over-year revenue growth in regions where it has stores.
Why it workedStaff use "buy in store and ship" every day, and the online team can hold local store stock for same-day pickup. New stores also brought more organic web traffic from nearby shoppers.
+10% sales
FROM FUTUREapparel · 15 stores. Sales up 10% from omnichannel features alone.
Why it workedStock used to be out of sync, so sales were lost when a size wasn't in the store. Now staff sell from one shared stock using ship-from-store or click-and-collect, and returns and exchanges work in any store or online.
+30% AOV
Bambi Baby. AOV and conversion both up 30% online and in store within 4 months.
Why it workedPaper checkout meant lines four or five deep and shoppers walking out. Mobile checkout anywhere on the floor cut it to seconds, which freed staff to spend time with shoppers and upsell. Loyalty points now apply automatically in store and online.
